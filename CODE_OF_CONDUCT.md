# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome here. Culm is about one thing: a good addon manager, built together and with fun.

## How we work together

- Be friendly and respectful, also when you disagree.
- Keep it about Culm: the code, the design, the bugs, the ideas.
- Give feedback that helps, and take feedback the same way. Criticise work, not people.
- Assume good intent; ask before you judge.

Insults, harassment, spam and personal attacks are not okay.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainer, [gengurke](https://github.com/gengurke), on GitHub. Reports are handled discreetly.
Comments, issues or contributions that break these rules may be edited or removed, and people who keep
breaking them may be blocked from the project.

This applies to everything around Culm: issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.
